Privacy Policy

RIFTLINE: LAST TRAIN (package com.riftline.lasttrain)
Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game RIFTLINE: LAST TRAIN (“the App”) handles information. It applies specifically to this App on Google Play and other platforms where we distribute it.

1. Summary

The App is a single-player game. It does not require an account, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not intentionally collect personal information for sale or marketing. Game progress is stored on your device.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment card details through the App.

On your device, the App may store:

  • Local game data — progress, settings, and similar save data needed to run the game.
  • Device vibration — if you enable haptics, the App may use the device vibrator. This does not send data off the device.

The App’s Android build does not request Internet permission for gameplay networking. If Google Play or the operating system provides install, crash, or integrity signals to Google as part of distributing or running apps from Play, that processing is governed by Google’s policies, not by us collecting that data inside the App.

3. How we use information

Local game data is used only to:

  • Save and restore your progress and preferences
  • Operate game features (including optional vibration feedback)

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell user data. We do not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers. We do not operate an in-app analytics or advertising network for this App.

If you contact us for support, any information you voluntarily send (for example an email) is used only to respond to your request.

5. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us (for example via a support email), contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local save data remains on your device until you clear the App’s storage, uninstall the App, or otherwise delete it using your device settings. We do not maintain a separate cloud copy of your progress for this App.

7. Security

Because progress is stored locally on your device, protect your device with the usual OS lock and update practices. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or the App:

Email: privacy@riftline.game
(If that address is not yet active, use the developer contact email listed on the Google Play store listing for RIFTLINE: LAST TRAIN.)

10. Your choices

  • Disable haptics in the App’s settings if you do not want vibration.
  • Clear App data or uninstall to remove local saves from the device.
